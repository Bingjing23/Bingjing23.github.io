PhD Candidate · UQ & QIMR Berghofer

Bingjing Zhou

I am a PhD candidate at The University of Queensland and QIMR Berghofer, affiliated with the Frazer Institute and the Genomics, Imaging, and AI Lab, respectively. I began my doctoral research in October 2024.

I am working on applying fMRI foundation models to large-scale brain imaging data to study the genetic basis of brain function and its links to disease. I also investigate shared brain imaging patterns between Alzheimer’s disease and its associated risk factors through brain-wide association analyses.

My doctoral research is supervised by Puya Gharahkhani, Miguel Rentería, and Baptiste Couvy-Duchesne. I also collaborate with Richard Hsieh and Santiago Diaz-Torres.

Alongside my PhD, I work as a research assistant at the Queensland Neurostimulation Centre (QNC).

Before starting my PhD, I completed my undergraduate degree in Computer Science and Technology and subsequently worked as a research assistant in the Department of Imaging and Interventional Radiology at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) from August 2023 to August 2024, working with James F. Griffith on deep learning for musculoskeletal MRI segmentation and automated quantification of inflammation in rheumatoid arthritis.
